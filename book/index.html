Book by Vincenzo Picciuolo

9600 BPS:
The Start.

Curiosity Never Had an Off Switch. I grew up in the last analog generation. This book follows the machines, music, people and questions that carried me into the digital world.

Paperback & Kindle · ASIN B0HF19FMGK

From dial up sound to artificial intelligence.

This is a personal story. It begins with electronics and a Commodore 64, moves through early networks, music and telecommunications, then reaches blockchain and AI. The technology changes, but the curiosity remains.

My generation remembers paper maps and GPS, letters and instant messages, cassette tapes and streaming. Living in both worlds changed how I see technology and what I expect from it.
